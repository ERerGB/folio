# Device transfer routes

Researched from vendor documentation on 2026-10-13. These are starting points, not promises for every model or firmware. Check the target model before delivery. `EPUB` below means an unprotected EPUB produced from the user's own content; DRM-protected purchases have different rules.

| Brand | Documented route for a personal EPUB | Where to verify | Vendor source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BOOX | BOOXDrop on the same LAN; USB and account-based Send2BOOX are alternatives. | BOOXDrop Receive/Library; then reader Library's directory or scan view. | [Computer transfer](https://help.boox.com/hc/en-us/articles/10701399613076-Transfer-with-Your-Computer), [Library display modes](https://help.boox.com/hc/en-us/articles/8569338166932-Choose-a-Book-Display-Mode) |
| Amazon Kindle | Send to Kindle accepts EPUB and synchronizes a personal document through Amazon's service. USB behavior depends on device and computer. | Kindle Library after processing and sync. | [Send to Kindle and USB guide](https://digprjsurvey.amazon.com/csad/help/node/TCUBEdEkbIhK07ysFu) |
| Rakuten Kobo | USB sideload of unprotected EPUB/PDF. Some models also support Google Drive or Dropbox; Google Drive requires a supported model and the Rakuten Kobo folder. | My Books after USB import or Drive download. | [USB import](https://help.kobo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024775093-Add-non-protected-PDF-and-ePub-files-to-your-Kobo-eReader-using-your-computer), [Google Drive models and steps](https://help.kobo.com/hc/en-us/articles/15335985512983-Add-books-to-your-eReader-using-Google-Drive) |
| PocketBook | Send-to-PocketBook email, PocketBook Cloud, Dropbox, or USB on supported devices. | Device Library or synchronized service folder. | [PocketBook device manual](https://support.pocketbook-int.com/fw/633/u/633.6.5.2915/manual/ww/User_Manual_Color_EN.pdf) |
| reMarkable | Desktop/mobile app, web app, or USB web interface imports EPUB. Web/USB limits and options differ. | The reMarkable document list after import and sync. | [Importing and exporting files](https://support.remarkable.com/articles/Knowledge/importing-and-exporting-files) |
| Supernote | Browse & Access exposes a local Wi-Fi browser upload; cloud, USB, and Partner app are alternatives. | Target folder on device; Partner app local transfers may land in INBOX. | [Transfer files](https://support.supernote.com/en_US/transfer-files) |
| tolino | Upload personal ebooks through tolino webreader/app into tolino cloud, then download to reader; USB also appears in device manuals. | My books / cloud item and downloaded copy on reader. | [tolino cloud FAQ](https://mytolino.com/faq/what-is-the-tolino-cloud-and-how-do-i-use-it-in-the-tolino-app/), [tolino manual](https://mytolino.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/tolino-epos3_Manual_EN.pdf) |

Transfer methods have different meaning: a file may exist on a computer, in a cloud account, in a device's storage, in its library index, or open in its reader. Report only the stage actually observed. An uploaded file is not automatically a visible home-screen book on every device. For cross-brand work, keep content and edition management shared, but choose a brand-specific delivery route at the end.
